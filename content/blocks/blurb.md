## See what needs updating, at a glance, consistently.

You get a consistent view of all package updates, regardless of host platform
or package manager, all in one place.

Targeted at small to medium sized networks, and designed to be simple to
deploy and use, requiring no central server, remote agents, or complex dependencies
installed on the hosts.

If you have SSH access to the hosts and your keypairs are loaded in an SSH
agent, you are good to go.
